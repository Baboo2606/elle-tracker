General Rules
⏰
Meal spacing: Eat every 3–4 hours. Times are a guide, intervals matter most.
🚫
No skipping: Never skip a meal. Make it up as soon as possible if you do.
💧
Water: At least 2,500ml (2.5L) per day. Add 500–750ml per hour of exercise.
☕
Coffee & tea: No sugar. Small milk for color. Stevia/sucralose OK. 100ml almond milk max/day.
😴
Sleep: Minimum 7 hours per night. Don't stay up for the last meal.
🧂
Seasoning OK: Pepper, turmeric, cinnamon, paprika, chili, cumin, tabasco, oregano. Reduce salt!
🫒
Cooking: No oil — cooking spray only.
